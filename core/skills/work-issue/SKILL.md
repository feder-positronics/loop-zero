# Work Issue

Use the issue for intake and the PR for delivery evidence. Read live acceptance,
comments, artifacts and links. Reconcile prior and partial work, branches and
competing PRs with [dispatch](../../../docs/DELEGATION.md#dispatch-authority).
Verify remote identity before mutation and environment before production claims.
Consult [approval evidence](reference.md#evidence-for-approval) for decision
counts, censuses, states or production approval, and [inherited work](reference.md#inherited-work)
before adopting a branch or PR. Keep its worktree when inherited. Link the issue
and follow [implement](../implement/SKILL.md), with safeguards for
[API shape](reference.md#api-shape-changes), [stack prerequisites or seeded browser state](reference.md#fresh-stack-proof)
and [repairs or implementation without a queue](reference.md#integration-without-a-queue).

Before publication, follow repo PR template/body policy and find the lifecycle
blueprint in the issue or its links. Cover required blueprint completion in
closing delivery. Children do not own parent blueprints. Before review, record
doc changes or why none apply. Before publication and merge, recheck and
reconcile collisions, live acceptance, prior delivery and ownership. Verify an
installed, working replacement before retiring a deployment workaround.
Name the unmet criterion or verified defect before a delta source edit.
Own review, CI and post-merge acceptance waits. Apply
[acceptance evidence](reference.md#acceptance-evidence) and [re-entry](reference.md#re-entry)
safeguards.

For complete acceptance use `Closes #N` in the task. Keep `Refs #N` for
[p0 owner orders and QA issues](../../../docs/DELEGATION.md#issue-closure-owners)
and partial or unobserved acceptance, including post-merge observation.
Check rendered descriptions and commits for unintended closure, even from
negated prose. Verify landing, SHA, cleanup, issue state and closing reference.
Follow safeguards for [premature closure](reference.md#premature-closure),
[late findings](reference.md#late-findings) and
[monitoring or deferred work](reference.md#monitoring-and-deferred-work).

Send evidence, PR/head or landed SHA, outstanding acceptance, next-action owner
and command or trigger to dispatch on the issue/PR or through the parent.
Reconcile conflicts and scope expansions or reserved choices with dispatch.
Continue only commissioned successors after recording evidence and checking
live state in a fresh task worktree.
